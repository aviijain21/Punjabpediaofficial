Why we exist

Coaching shouldn't be the price of a government job

For most aspirants in Punjab, clearing an exam means paying for coaching — because the books alone are never quite enough. Something is always missing: the previous year papers, the current data, the practice questions that match the real paper.

Punjab Pedia exists to close that gap. Every book is built to be complete enough on its own that a dedicated student can prepare entirely by self-study and walk into the exam hall fully ready.

How we do it

Built from Punjab's own papers

Every title is prepared from papers actually set by the boards that recruit in Punjab — PSSSB, PPSC and the agencies that conduct their exams. Nothing generic, nothing padded with material Punjab never asks.

And because a large share of Punjab's aspirants studied in Punjabi medium, our books are published in English and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ together, so you can prepare in the language you think in.
