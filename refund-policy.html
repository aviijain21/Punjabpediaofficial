Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel any time before your order is dispatched by contacting us through our contact page. You will be refunded in full.
  • Once dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled, but it may qualify for return under the terms below.

2. Returns and replacements

  • If a book arrives damaged, defective (missing or misprinted pages), or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery and send photographs of the book and the packaging.
  • Once verified, we will send a free replacement or refund you in full — your choice.
  • Because books are printed copyrighted material, we cannot accept returns for reasons other than damage, defect or a wrong item being sent.

3. How refunds are processed

  • Approved refunds are initiated within 3–5 working days.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through our payment gateway. Depending on your bank, the money may take a further 5–10 working days to appear.

4. Orders placed on Amazon

For books bought on Amazon.in, Amazon's own return and refund policy applies to that order.

5. Contact

To request a refund or cancellation, contact us through our contact page with your order details.
